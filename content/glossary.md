---
title: Glossary
standfirst: "Sample terms shown below — the full glossary from the book will replace this list."
terms:
  - title: Blade
    body: The flat, expanded part of a fern leaf (frond).
  - title: Frond
    body: The leaf of a fern, comprising a stipe and a blade.
  - title: Indusium
    body: A thin membrane that covers and protects a developing sorus.
  - title: Pinna
    body: A primary division of a fern blade, plural pinnae.
  - title: Rhizome
    body: The often horizontal stem from which fronds and roots arise.
  - title: Sorus
    body: A cluster of spore-producing sporangia on the underside of a frond, plural sori.
  - title: Stipe
    body: The stalk of a fern frond, below the blade.

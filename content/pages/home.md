This website is the official companion site to the book *The Ferns and Lycophytes of Texas* by George Diggs, Jr., and Barney Lipscomb (published February 2014 by Botanical Research Institute of Texas Press).

Throughout this site you will find information, plant descriptions, and artwork drawn straight from the book, along with additional information derived from the herbarium collections at the Botanical Research Institute of Texas (BRIT) in Fort Worth, Texas, as well as other features created by BRIT's botanical staff — such as the visual key — in collaboration with the book's authors.
